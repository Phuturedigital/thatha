Thatha Basic

Affordable. Practical.
Built for every trader.

The mass-market unit. Designed for institutional scale without losing dignity, portability or local repairability — a simplified, tool-free construction that avoids expensive custom runners and makes individual parts easy to replace.

  • Flat-pack transport
  • Tool-free slot-and-pin assembly
  • Replaceable modular panels
  • Optional canopy and wheel kit
  • Detachable sponsor branding
Enquire about Basic
Thatha Plus

More counter.
More to sell.

For the trader who has outgrown the starting point. Plus adds working surface, shelving and shade — the three things traders ask for first once volume picks up.

  • Extended counter and side table
  • Internal shelving for stock
  • Umbrella mount and shade package
  • Larger branding and advertising panels
  • Wheel kit for daily transport
Enquire about Plus
Thatha Pro

The full trading floor.

The large-format unit for established traders, food businesses and event operators. Lockable storage means stock stays with the unit overnight, and the heavier frame carries cooking and refrigeration modules.

  • Largest trading and display area
  • Lockable overnight storage
  • Food and beverage module ready
  • Heavy-duty frame and wheels
  • Suited to events and corporate activations
