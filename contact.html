Before you ask

Common questions

Can I buy a unit today?

Not yet. THATHA is at pre-production stage — the design and range are defined, but units are not in manufacture. Register your interest and we will come back to you when the first production run is scheduled.

Are the prices on this site final?

No. Every price shown is an indicative planning target pending prototype sign-off, bill-of-materials costing and manufacturer quotation. A binding price needs a formal quotation.

Do you deliver outside South Africa?

Not at this stage. The initial focus is South Africa, where the product is designed to be manufactured and repaired locally. We are happy to talk to partners elsewhere about later expansion.

I represent a municipality or NGO. Where do I start?

Choose “Government / enterprise programme” above and tell us the budget, site and beneficiary count. See the programmes page for what a rollout includes.

Can I advertise on THATHA units?

Yes — detachable sponsor panels are designed into every unit. This becomes available once units are deployed; choose “Partnership or distribution” and we will keep you posted.
