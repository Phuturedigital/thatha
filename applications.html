Why it adapts

Modularity is
the whole point

A trader's business changes with the season, the site and the customer. A unit that only does one thing becomes dead capital the moment that thing stops selling.

01

One frame, many modules

The base structure never changes. Counters, coolers, shelving and rails attach to the same sockets, so switching trade is an accessory purchase, not a new unit.

02

Repairable in the township

Standard panels and low-cost pins mean a damaged part is replaced locally in minutes. No proprietary hardware, no waiting on an importer.

03

Branding that comes off

Sponsor panels detach without altering the unit — so a funded programme can be branded for its term and handed on cleanly afterwards.
