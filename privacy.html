Privacy &
POPIA notice

This notice explains what personal information THATHA collects through this website, why it is used and how you can exercise your rights.

Effective: 2 November 2026

Responsible party

Who handles your information

THATHA is the responsible party for enquiries submitted through this website. Privacy requests can be sent to hello@thathamove.org.za or made by calling 078 458 3099.

Information we collect

The enquiry form may collect your name, organisation, email address, phone number, province, approximate number of units required, enquiry category and the message you choose to provide. Supplying an email address and name is necessary if you want a response. Other fields are voluntary.

The website host may also process ordinary technical information such as IP address, browser type, request time and security logs so the site can be delivered and protected.

Why we use it

  • To respond to your product, sponsorship, programme, partnership or investment enquiry.
  • To prepare information or a quotation you requested.
  • To maintain a record of the conversation and follow up on the requested opportunity.
  • To protect the website, prevent spam and meet legal obligations.

Submitting an enquiry does not subscribe you to general marketing. THATHA will ask separately before sending unrelated promotional communication.

Who receives it

Authorised THATHA personnel and service providers needed to operate the website may receive the information. The current form-delivery provider is FormSubmit, and the website is hosted by Vercel. These providers process information to deliver the form and website services. THATHA does not sell personal information.

Some service providers may process information outside South Africa. Where that happens, THATHA will use providers and contractual arrangements intended to provide appropriate protection.

How long we keep it

Enquiry records are kept only as long as reasonably needed to answer the request, pursue the opportunity, meet legal or accounting requirements, or resolve a dispute. Enquiries that do not lead to an active relationship should normally be reviewed for deletion after 24 months.

Your POPIA rights

You may ask whether THATHA holds personal information about you, request access to it, ask for inaccurate information to be corrected, object to certain processing, withdraw consent where consent is the basis for processing, or request deletion where retention is no longer justified. THATHA may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Email hello@thathamove.org.za with the subject “POPIA request.” If you believe your information has been handled unlawfully, you may also contact the Information Regulator South Africa or email POPIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za.

Security and updates

THATHA uses reasonable technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the information handled. No internet service can promise absolute security. This notice will be updated if the form, service providers, purposes or business structure materially change.
