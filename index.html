Simple by design

Slide. Lock. Trade.

The mass-market model is built around a reliable slot-and-pin system rather than costly mechanical hardware — so it is cheaper to make, faster to set up and easier to repair locally.

01

Open

Release the transport strap and unfold the base and side panels.

02

Slot

Guide the panels and supports into their pre-cut sockets.

03

Lock

Insert the low-cost locking pins and check that every support is secure.

04

Raise

Fit the canopy frame or the optional umbrella mount.

05

Brand

Attach trader or sponsor panels without permanently altering the unit.

06

Trade

Add the module for your trade and start operating in minutes.
