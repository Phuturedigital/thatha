Our mission

Opportunity,
made mobile.

To empower entrepreneurs with affordable, portable and scalable trading infrastructure that makes starting and growing a business more accessible.

THATHA provides practical solutions that enable individuals, communities and organisations to create professional and sustainable trading opportunities.

The brand is built on the belief that entrepreneurship should not be limited by location, resources or access to infrastructure. With THATHA, opportunity becomes mobile, adaptable and within reach.

What the name means

Thatha means “take” in isiZulu and isiXhosa. It is an instruction and an invitation at the same time: take the opportunity, take the unit, take your trade further.

What we make

  • Portable, foldable, adaptable retail structures for entrepreneurs
  • Infrastructure supporting government, NGO and corporate entrepreneurship programmes
  • Custom retail platforms for specific trades
  • Tools that help businesses start, operate and grow

The mark

People, not
products.

The mark is built from abstract humanoid forms — people at the heart of the brand. Connected around a central structure, the figures stand for community, collaboration and shared growth.

The balanced geometric form conveys stability. The outward movement reflects progress, opportunity and empowerment. It is a modern geometric mark for a portable, scalable product: interconnected, forward-moving, adaptable.

A note on type.

The THATHA identity specifies Benton Sans. Because it is a licensed typeface that cannot be embedded on a public website, this demo renders in Public Sans and Archivo — chosen because both descend from the same early-twentieth-century Franklin Gothic lineage as Benton Sans, so the letterforms sit close to the original. A production build would licence Benton Sans as a webfont.
