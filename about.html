Why THATHA exists

A business can start
before a building does.

Across South Africa, entrepreneurs sell food, drinks, produce and everyday goods wherever customers gather: taxi ranks, pavements, schools, workplaces and events.

The business idea is often ready before the infrastructure is. Traders improvise counters, shelter and storage, then pack everything away at the end of the day. THATHA turns those separate problems into one portable platform that can be moved, repaired, branded and upgraded as the business grows.

What we do

The name

Take the opportunity.
Take your trade further.

Thatha means “take” in isiZulu and isiXhosa. The name is an invitation to act: take the unit, take your place in the market and take the next step in your business.

“The unit is useful only when it helps a person trade with more confidence, consistency and room to grow.”
